The mean of the numeric sample is about 0.075. The mean of the logical
column is 0.5 because R counts TRUE as 1 and FALSE as 0; five of the ten
values are TRUE. The means of the character and factor columns return NA
because their values are categories rather than numbers. With
as.numeric(), the logical values become 1s and 0s, while the letters
become NA because they cannot be read as numbers. The factor becomes
numeric codes for its three levels, but those codes are not
measurements, so averaging them would not be meaningful.
